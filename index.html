Person 1 · step 7 · Cholesky

Cholesky: the square root of a matrix

Machine

Like 9 = 3 × 3, find a triangular L with A = L Lᵀ. Recipe: root the corner, divide down, root what's left.

The smoothie trick: independent in, correlated out

Say“Cholesky is a square root for matrices. Why care? It's the recipe for random numbers that move together, like dads' and sons' heights. Start with independent random numbers, multiply by L, and now they're correlated: separate ingredients in, smoothie out.” Hit Blend.
Person 1 · step 8 · inverse

The inverse is the undo button

Machine
In plain EnglishSwap the diagonal, flip the signs of the other two, divide by the determinant. Machine then undo = do nothing. If the determinant is 0, you can't un-squash a pancake.

Run the machine, then undo it

Say“The inverse is the undo button: swap, flip, divide. Machine then undo gives the do-nothing matrix.” Try the [1 1; 1 1] preset to show the pancake: determinant 0, no undo.

Person 2 · the stand

Our lemonade stand sells two drinks

Prices are in cents per cup. Sell more of a drink, and you have to price it lower. The drinks are substitutes: lots of iced tea steals lemonade customers. And both drinks share one pitcher, so making more of one makes the other more expensive.

Run the stand

Say“I got the business one, so let's make it a lemonade stand. Prices drop the more you sell, the drinks steal each other's customers, and they share one pitcher. Multiply it out: profit = 50q₁ + 40q₂ − 2q₁² − 2q₂² − 2q₁q₂. The professor's hint, ‘it's a quadratic form,’ just means squares and times-each-other terms packed into a little grid.”

If someone asks…Say
Spectral vs. Cholesky?Spectral shows the shape: which directions stretch, by how much. Cholesky is what computers use to make correlated random numbers.
Why are the special directions at a right angle?Because the matrix is a mirror image across its diagonal (symmetric).
What if he gives a different matrix?Same recipe: trace and determinant, then λ² − trace·λ + det = 0. Type it into this lab and every slide updates.
Why are bell-curve contours never X-shaped?Variance can't be negative, so that matrix is always a bowl.
What if the drinks were burgers and fries?Complements: selling one raises the other's price, so the cross numbers flip sign and the hill changes shape.
